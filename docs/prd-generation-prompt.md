## Brainstorming summary

**What we are going to build**
Review Buddy: a platform where makers submit their products and receive structured, actionable feedback
(what reviewers liked / what can be improved), with an AI-generated summary of all reviews for each product.

**Vision / goal**
Help makers improve their products through honest, structured feedback instead of upvotes and one-line comments.

**Problem**
Existing platforms (Product Hunt, Show HN, Reddit) are built for launch visibility, not for improvement.
Makers rarely get feedback they can act on.

**Target users**
- Makers: indie hackers and small teams who want feedback on their product
- Reviewers: other makers and early adopters who enjoy giving feedback and want to build a reputation

**MVP scope**
- Submit a product
- Leave a structured review: liked / can be improved
- AI summary of all reviews for a product
- Browse products and reviews

**Out of scope for now**
Payments, featured listings, advanced moderation, mobile app.

**Success signal**
Makers who receive reviews come back to submit another product or review someone else's product.
