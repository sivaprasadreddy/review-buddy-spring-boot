# Review Buddy — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Overview

Review Buddy is a platform where makers submit their products and receive structured, actionable feedback from other makers and early adopters.

Products are publicly visible to everyone. Reviewers provide feedback in two simple areas: what they liked and what could be improved. Review Buddy generates an AI summary of the reviews for each product to help makers understand the overall feedback.

## 2. Problem Statement

Existing platforms such as Product Hunt, Show HN, and Reddit are primarily designed for launch visibility, discussion, and discovery. They tend to generate upvotes, reactions, and short comments rather than structured feedback that helps makers improve their products.

Makers need a simple way to collect honest feedback that is organized around what works and what could be better.

## 3. Goal / Vision

Help makers improve their products through honest, structured feedback instead of relying primarily on upvotes and one-line comments.

Review Buddy should create a feedback loop where makers receive useful reviews and reviewers contribute meaningful feedback to products.

## 4. Target Users

- **Makers:** Indie hackers and small teams who want actionable feedback on their products.
- **Reviewers:** Other makers and early adopters who enjoy giving feedback and want to build a reputation through their reviews.

## 5. Key Features (MVP)

- Submit a product for public feedback.
- Leave a structured review describing what was liked and what could be improved.
- Generate an AI summary of all reviews for each product.
- Browse publicly available products and their reviews.

## 6. Out of Scope

- Payments.
- Featured listings.
- Advanced moderation.
- Mobile app.

## 7. Success Metrics

- Makers who receive reviews return to submit another product.
- Makers who receive reviews return to review someone else's product.
- Products receive meaningful reviews rather than only being submitted and viewed.

## 8. Risks and Assumptions

- **Risk:** Reviews may be too generic or lack actionable detail.
- **Risk:** Public products may attract low-quality or unhelpful feedback.
- **Risk:** AI-generated summaries may fail to accurately represent the underlying reviews.
- **Assumption:** Makers are willing to give feedback to other products as well as receive feedback on their own.
- **Assumption:** Public visibility is acceptable for makers submitting products.
- **Assumption:** The combination of structured reviews and summarized feedback provides more value for product improvement than simple votes or comments.

## 9. Open Questions

- What information should makers provide when submitting a product?
- Is authentication required to submit products or leave reviews?
- Can makers review their own products?
- Should reviewers be able to edit or delete their reviews?
- Should makers be able to respond to reviews?
- How should products with no reviews be presented?
- How should AI summaries be handled when there are only a small number of reviews?
- What mechanisms, if any, are needed to prevent spam or abusive reviews in the MVP?
- What constitutes a meaningful review for measuring MVP success?
